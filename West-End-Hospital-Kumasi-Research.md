## Identity — CONFIRMED

- **Name:** West End Hospital (also written "WEST END HOSPITAL, Edwenase Kwadaso" on Google Maps).
- **Locally known as:** "Kwakye-Maafo Hospital" — after its founder, Dr. J.K. Kwakye-Maafo (Wikipedia, Services & Places, Agoo). This is a real local nickname, safe to mention.
- **Type:** Private, general hospital. 40 beds (Wikipedia infobox, GhanaYello, Agoo, Services & Places — all agree).
- **Founded:** 1978 as a clinic; grown into a hospital by 1985 (Wikipedia hospital article). NOTE a small conflict: the founder's biography says "In 1979, Kwakye-Maafo with the help of his wife Mercy established the West End Clinic". Use 1978 (hospital article + infobox "Opened 1978") and do not make a fuss of the one-year gap.
- **Named after:** the Westend Klinikum in West Berlin, Germany.
- **Founder:** Dr. Jacob Kenneth Kofi Kwakye-Maafo (born 1940), physician and surgeon specialising in Obstetrics and Gynaecology, former president of the Ghana Medical Association, chief executive of the hospital, also Nana Ohemeng Awere V (chief of Assin Nsuta). He founded the clinic in 1978/79 with his wife Mercy. Wikipedia's founder bio says "He still runs West End Hospital" — but that article is undated and he is 86; do NOT present him as the current day-to-day chief executive without confirmation. His wife is Mercy Kwakye-Maafo née **Mercy Golightly**, a retired general and psychiatric nurse — the Out-Patients Department is named the **"Mercy Golightly Unit"** after her (confirmed by a Google Maps photo of the OPD sign).
- **Staff:** "over 40 staff" (Wikipedia).
- **NHIS:** Accredited (Wikipedia infobox "Standards: NHIS accredited"). Also confirmed by the hospital's own sign: the entrance board lists an "NHIS OFFICE" on site (Google Maps photo).
- **Emergency department:** Yes (Wikipedia infobox). Also confirmed by the hospital's own sign: "ACCIDENTS AND EMERGENCY" (Google Maps photo).

## VERDICT

**YES — a good no-website lead, arguably one of the strongest so far.**

Why:
- Established, well-known private hospital (founded 1978, 40 beds, NHIS-accredited, 24h, emergency department) — a serious business, not a small clinic.
- Its only website, westend-hospital.com, is confirmed DEAD today (DNS resolves, server does not answer; no spam, no hijack — a clean rebuild job).
- No Facebook, no Instagram, no email anywhere — the hospital is effectively invisible online beyond directory listings, so a website is a clear, easy pitch.
- Real, verifiable content to build with: a named founder with a strong reputation (former Ghana Medical Association president, famed for fertility/OB-GYN), a named OPD unit (Mercy Golightly Unit), a photographed services board, a real building photo, and a 4.2-star Google rating.
- Contact route is clear: phone is the way in (call 024 546 0006 first — the number Google Maps itself shows; landline 032 202 2590 as backup).

- CONFIRMED: name, 40 beds, private, NHIS-accredited, 24h, emergency dept, founded 1978 (clinic) / hospital by 1985, founder and his story, local nickname "Kwakye-Maafo Hospital", services list above (Wikipedia + photographed signboard), phones 032 202 2590 and 024 546 0006, P.O.
